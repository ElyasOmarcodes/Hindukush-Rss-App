Privacy Policy — Hindukush Ghag

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hindukush Ghag mobile and desktop application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Hindukush Ghag is a free news reader that displays publicly available content from the Hindukush Ghag websites. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information we do not collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. We do not sell or share any personal data, because we do not collect any.

2. Information stored only on your device

To work offline and remember your preferences, the App stores the following locally on your device only (it is never transmitted to us):

  • Cached articles and images you have viewed, so they load quickly and work offline.
  • Your saved / favorite articles.
  • Which articles you have read, and items you chose to delete from your view.
  • Your settings: language, theme, accent color, text size, and retention preferences.

You can clear all of this at any time from Settings → Clear database, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Network requests

To show you the news, the App fetches publicly available content directly from the Hindukush Ghag websites:

  • hindukushpa.com (Pashto)
  • hindokosh.com (Dari)
  • hindukushen.com (English)

These requests are standard web requests. Article images and video thumbnails (including YouTube preview images) are loaded from their respective servers. When you choose to open an article, video, or website link, it opens using your device's browser or the relevant app, which is governed by that provider's own privacy policy.

4. Third-party services

The App itself contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries. If you tap a video, you may be taken to a third-party platform (such as YouTube); your use of that platform is subject to its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Notifications

If enabled in a future version, notifications about new articles would be handled with your explicit permission and can be turned off at any time in your device settings or in the App's settings.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a general news reader and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Data security

Because data is stored only on your device and we operate no user servers, your reading data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share an article using your device's share feature.

8. Your choices

  • Disable offline storage in Settings.
  • Clear all stored data in Settings → Clear database.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the App and on our website with an updated "Last updated" date.
